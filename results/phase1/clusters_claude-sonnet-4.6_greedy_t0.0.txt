## The Grief Cartographer

A mobile app that lets users map their grief spatially rather than chronologically. Instead of journaling through time, users pin emotional memories to an interactive, abstract landscape that shifts and evolves — mountains form where grief feels heavy, rivers carve through areas of acceptance, fog settles over unresolved feelings. Over months, the map transforms visually as the user processes loss, creating a living, personal geography of healing.
